## 1. Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) — Bhuvan Platform

**Source**: ISRO Bhuvan Open Data Archive
**URL**: https://bhuvan.nrsc.gov.in
**License**: ISRO Open Data Policy (Government of India)

### Datasets Used
- **CartoDEM v3.1** — Digital Elevation Model at 30m resolution
- **LISS-III Imagery** — Resourcesat-2/2A multispectral (23.5m, 4 bands)
- **LISS-IV Imagery** — Resourcesat-2/2A high-resolution (5.8m, 3 bands)
- **AWiFS Imagery** — Resourcesat-2/2A wide field (56m, 4 bands)
- **RISAT-1 SAR** — C-band Synthetic Aperture Radar (3-50m)
- **Resourcesat-2/2A** — Multi-sensor Indian remote sensing satellite
- **Landslide Atlas** — National landslide inventory (vector)
- **Flood Monitoring Data** — Flood extent mapping products (vector)
- **Thematic Maps** — Land Use/Land Cover and Geomorphology maps

### Access Methods
- Web Coverage Service (WCS) for raster imagery
- Web Feature Service (WFS) for vector datasets
- Web Map Service (WMS) for thematic maps
- Open Data Archive (ODA) for bulk downloads

### Citation
> Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). Bhuvan — Indian Geo-Platform of ISRO. National Remote Sensing Centre, Hyderabad. Available at: https://bhuvan.nrsc.gov.in

## 2. India Meteorological Department (IMD)

**Source**: IMD Pune Data Supply Portal
**URL**: https://dsp.imdpune.gov.in
**License**: IMD Open Data Policy (Government of India)

### Datasets Used
- **Station-wise Rainfall Data** — Daily rainfall from ~20 NER stations
- **Station-wise Temperature Data** — Daily min/max temperature from NER stations
- **0.25-degree Gridded Rainfall** — Spatially interpolated daily rainfall (binary format)
- **Extreme Weather Events** — Heavy/very heavy rainfall event catalog

### Citation
> India Meteorological Department (IMD), Ministry of Earth Sciences, Government of India. Climate Data Products. IMD Pune Data Supply Portal. Available at: https://dsp.imdpune.gov.in

## 5. Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem (CDSE)

**Source**: European Space Agency / European Commission
**URL**: https://dataspace.copernicus.eu
**License**: Copernicus Sentinel Data — Free, Full, and Open Access

### Datasets Used
- **Sentinel-2 L2A** — Surface reflectance multispectral (10-60m, 13 bands)
- **Sentinel-2 L1C** — Top-of-atmosphere reflectance (10-60m, 13 bands)
- **Sentinel-1 GRD** — Ground Range Detected SAR (10m, VV+VH)
- **Sentinel-1 SLC** — Single Look Complex SAR (5x20m, VV+VH)
- **Copernicus DEM GLO-30** — Global 30m Digital Elevation Model
- **Copernicus EMS** — Emergency Management Service activation maps

### Known NER-Related EMS Activations
- EMSR504, EMSR522, EMSR567, EMSR612, EMSR634, EMSR678, EMSR701, EMSR745

### Access Methods
- CDSE OData API (primary)
- sentinelsat library (fallback)

### Citation
> European Space Agency (ESA). Copernicus Sentinel Data [2020-2026]. Accessed via Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem. Available at: https://dataspace.copernicus.eu. Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data.

> Copernicus Emergency Management Service (EMS). Emergency Management Service — Mapping. Available at: https://emergency.copernicus.eu
